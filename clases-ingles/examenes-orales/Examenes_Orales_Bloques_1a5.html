Exámenes orales finales · Bloques 1 a 5

Clase privada · Jerónimo (10) y Luciana (9) · Nivel A1+ → A2 · Profe Camilo Espinosa

Cómo usarlo. Cada niño responde solo, uno a la vez; el otro puede esperar jugando o escuchando con audífonos. Cada bloque toma unos 10 a 12 minutos por niño.

Orden de cada examen: 1) Calentamiento (preguntas personales, para que se sientan seguros), 2) Preguntas del bloque, 3) Mini charla de 1 minuto, 4) Puntaje.

Si se bloquea: repite la pregunta más despacio, señala un objeto o da la primera palabra ("I like..."). Si respondió con ayuda, márcalo como "con apoyo" (1 punto en vez de 2).

Debajo de cada pregunta hay un ejemplo de respuesta. Es una guía: cualquier respuesta correcta y con sentido vale.

Bloque 1 · Me and My World

S1 Presentation · S2 Question Master · S3 Describe It! · S5 My Day · S6 Your Day vs My Day · S7 I Like It Because · S8 Show

1. Calentamiento

  1. What's your name? How old are you?My name is Jerónimo. I am ten years old.
  2. Where do you live? What school do you go to?I live in ... I go to ...

2. Preguntas del bloque

  1. Tell me about you. Who are you? S1I am Luciana. I am nine years old. I like dogs and music.
  2. What is your favorite food? Your favorite color? Your favorite animal? S1–S2My favorite food is pizza. My favorite color is blue.
  3. Now you ask me 2 questions. Use "Do you like...?" and "What is your favorite...?" S2Do you like soccer? What is your favorite movie? (evalúa que forme bien la pregunta)
  4. Look at this (muestra algo grande y algo pequeño). Describe it. S3It is big and blue. It is small and fast. (opuestos: big/small, fast/slow, hot/cold, heavy/light)
  5. What do you do in the morning? Tell me your routine. S5First I wake up. Then I brush my teeth. After that I eat breakfast. Then I get dressed and I go to school.
  6. What time do you wake up? How do you go to school? S5–S6I wake up at six. I go to school by car.
  7. Tell me about your sister / brother (o un amigo). What does he/she do every day? S6She wakes up at seven. She goes to school. She plays the piano. (fíjate en la -s: wakes, goes, plays)
  8. Does Luciana (Jero) like pizza? Does she play soccer? S6Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't.
  9. What do you like? Why? S7I like soccer because it is fun.
  10. Pizza or hamburgers? Why? S7I like hamburgers because they are delicious.

3. Mini charla (1 minuto)

"Tell me about you and your day." Debe incluir: quién es, qué le gusta y por qué, y su rutina con first / then.

Bloque 2 · My World Around Me

S9 Where Is It? · S10 There Is / There Are · S11 My Dream Room · S12 Review · S13 Giving Directions · S14 My Neighborhood · S15 Bigger, Smaller, Better · Bonus The Right Order · S16 Tour Guide Show

1. Calentamiento

  1. How are you today? What did you do in the game "My World"?I'm fine. I gave directions to a tourist.
  2. Do you live in a house or an apartment?I live in a house.

2. Preguntas del bloque

  1. Where is your bag? Where is the pencil? (pon objetos: en, sobre, debajo, al lado) S9The pencil is on the table. My bag is under the chair. (in, on, under, next to, between, behind)
  2. (Esconde un objeto) Where is the ball now? S9It is behind the sofa. It is between the book and the lamp.
  3. What is there in your bedroom? S10There is a bed. There are two lamps. There is a TV next to the window. (is + uno, are + varios)
  4. Is there a TV in your room? Are there any books? S10Yes, there is. / No, there isn't. Yes, there are many books.
  5. Describe your dream room. S11In my dream room there is a big bed. There is a guitar next to my bed. There are three robots.
  6. What places are there in your neighborhood? S14There is a park, a supermarket and a school.
  7. Give me directions from here to the kitchen (o al baño, a la puerta). S13Go straight. Turn left. Stop! The kitchen is next to the living room. (go straight, turn left/right, stop, go back, cross)
  8. Where is the bakery? (muestra el mapa del juego o uno dibujado) S14The bakery is next to the park. / It is between the bank and the school.
  9. Compare: an elephant and a dog. A car and a bike. Pizza and broccoli. S15The elephant is bigger than the dog. A car is faster / more expensive than a bike. Pizza is better than broccoli.
  10. Who is taller, you or your sister/brother? Who is older? S15I am taller than Luciana. Jero is older than me.
  11. Describe this (un objeto) with 3 or 4 words. Remember the order. BonusA beautiful big old blue sofa. (Opinion → Size → Age → Color → noun)

3. Mini charla (1 minuto)

"You are a tour guide. Show me your world." Debe incluir: lo que hay en su casa (there is / there are), dónde están las cosas (preposiciones), cómo llegar a un lugar (direcciones) y una comparación.

Bloque 3 · My Stories

S5 Yesterday! · S6 Irregular Rebels · S7 What Happened? · S8 Review · S9 First, Then, Finally · S10 My Best Day · S11–S12 Show

1. Calentamiento

  1. What day is it today? What did you do today?Today is ... I went to school.
  2. Did you play the game "My Stories"? Who ate the cake?Yes, I did. Ana ate the cake!

2. Preguntas del bloque

  1. What did you do yesterday? S5Yesterday I played soccer and I watched a movie. (-ed: played, watched, cooked, walked)
  2. Say it in the past: play, watch, cook, clean, dance. S5played, watched, cooked, cleaned, danced
  3. Now the rebels: go, eat, see, have, is. S6went, ate, saw, had, was (atención: no "goed", "eated", "seed")
  4. Where did you go last weekend? What did you eat? S6I went to the park. I ate ice cream.
  5. Was it hot or cold yesterday? Were your friends at school? S6It was hot. Yes, they were. (was con I/he/she/it, were con you/we/they)
  6. Now you are the detective: ask me 3 questions about my weekend. S7Did you go to the beach? What did you eat? Where did you go? (did + verbo en presente)
  7. Did you see a movie yesterday? Did you eat pizza? S7Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.
  8. (Muestra 4 dibujos desordenados o di 4 acciones) Put the story in order and tell it. S9First, I woke up. Then, I went to the beach. After that, I swam. Finally, I went home.
  9. Tell me about your best day. S10My best day was my birthday. First, my friends came. Then, we ate cake. Finally, we played games. It was amazing!
  10. Why was it your best day? How did you feel? S10It was my best day because ... I was very happy.

3. Mini charla (2 minutos, como en S11)

"Who are you, what is your world like, and what was your best day?" Debe incluir: presentarse (Bloque 1), describir su casa o barrio (Bloque 2) y contar su mejor día en pasado con first, then, after that, finally. Si quieres, cuenta las muletillas ("ehh", "umm") como en S11.

Bloque 4 · The World is Mine

S13 What Are You Doing? · S14 Right Now vs Every Day · S15 What's Going to Happen? · S16 Grammar Tournament · S17 Can You? · S18 You Should · S19–S20 Dreams and Future Plans · S21 The World Around Us · S22 Ultimate Challenge · S23–S24 Show

1. Calentamiento

  1. How are you? What are you doing right now?I'm fine. I am talking with you.
  2. What is your favorite game in "The Time Travel Stage"?My favorite is ... because ...

2. Preguntas del bloque

  1. (Haz una mímica: correr, comer, bailar) What am I doing? S13You are running. You are eating. You are dancing. (am/is/are + -ing)
  2. What is your mom (dad, sister) doing right now? S13She is working. He is cooking.
  3. What do you do every day? And what are you doing now? S14Every day I go to school. Right now I am speaking English. (every day = presente simple; right now = -ing)
  4. What are you going to do this weekend? Tomorrow? S15I am going to play soccer. Tomorrow I am going to visit my grandma.
  5. What can you do? What can't you do? S17I can swim. I can't fly.
  6. Ask me for permission. (Ej.: usar el celular, ir al baño) S17Can I use the phone, please? Can I go to the bathroom?
  7. I have a headache. I am tired. I am hungry. What should I do? S18You should rest. You should sleep. You should eat something. You shouldn't play video games.
  8. What do you want to be in the future? Why? S19–S20I want to be a doctor because I want to help people. I am going to study a lot.
  9. Beach or mountains? City or countryside? Why? S21I prefer the beach because I love the sea.
  10. Mix: What did you do yesterday, what are you doing now, and what are you going to do tomorrow? S16, S22Yesterday I played. Now I am talking. Tomorrow I am going to swim. (pasado, presente continuo y futuro en una sola respuesta)

3. Mini charla (2 minutos)

"Your past, your present and your future." Debe incluir: algo que hizo (pasado), lo que hace cada día y lo que está haciendo ahora (presente), un sueño o un plan (futuro) y un consejo con should.

Bloque 5 · I Am Ready

Conectores trabajados en clase: but, so, because, however, although. Del Bloque 5 solo tengo estos conectores; ajusta las preguntas a lo que viste en cada sesión.

1. Calentamiento

  1. How do you feel today? Why?I feel happy because it is Friday.
  2. What is your favorite thing about English class?I like the games because they are fun.

2. Preguntas del bloque

  1. Why do you like your favorite sport (food, game)? becauseI like soccer because I play with my friends.
  2. Finish my sentence: "I like pizza, but..." butI like pizza, but I don't like broccoli.
  3. Tell me something you can do and something you can't. Use "but". butI can swim, but I can't skate.
  4. Finish: "It was raining, so..." soIt was raining, so I stayed at home.
  5. You were very hungry. What did you do? Use "so". soI was hungry, so I ate a sandwich.
  6. Finish: "I wanted to play outside. However,..." howeverHowever, it was raining.
  7. Finish: "Although I was tired,..." althoughAlthough I was tired, I finished my homework.
  8. Join the two ideas: "I studied a lot." + "The test was difficult." although / howeverAlthough I studied a lot, the test was difficult. / I studied a lot. However, the test was difficult.
  9. Which word: "I was sick, ___ I went to the doctor." soso (y que diga la oración completa)
  10. Tell me about your year in English. What was easy? What was difficult? todosThe games were easy because I like them, but the past tense was difficult. However, now I can tell stories.

3. Mini charla final del año (2 a 3 minutos)

"I am ready! Tell me about you and your year." Debe incluir: quién es y su mundo (Bloques 1 y 2), una historia en pasado (Bloque 3), su presente y su futuro (Bloque 4), y al menos tres conectores distintos (because, but, so, however, although).
