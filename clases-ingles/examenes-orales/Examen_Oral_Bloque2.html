Clase privada · Jerónimo (10) y Luciana (9) · Nivel A1+ → A2 · Profe Camilo Espinosa

Cómo usarlo. Cada niño responde solo, uno a la vez; el otro puede esperar jugando o escuchando con audífonos. Cada bloque toma unos 10 a 12 minutos por niño.

Orden de cada examen: 1) Calentamiento (preguntas personales, para que se sientan seguros), 2) Preguntas del bloque, 3) Mini charla de 1 minuto, 4) Puntaje.

Si se bloquea: repite la pregunta más despacio, señala un objeto o da la primera palabra ("I like..."). Si respondió con ayuda, márcalo como "con apoyo" (1 punto en vez de 2).

Debajo de cada pregunta hay un ejemplo de respuesta. Es una guía: cualquier respuesta correcta y con sentido vale.

Bloque 2 · My World Around Me

S9 Where Is It? · S10 There Is / There Are · S11 My Dream Room · S12 Review · S13 Giving Directions · S14 My Neighborhood · S15 Bigger, Smaller, Better · Bonus The Right Order · S16 Tour Guide Show

1. Calentamiento

  1. How are you today? What did you do in the game "My World"?I'm fine. I gave directions to a tourist.
  2. Do you live in a house or an apartment?I live in a house.

2. Preguntas del bloque

  1. Where is your bag? Where is the pencil? (pon objetos: en, sobre, debajo, al lado) S9The pencil is on the table. My bag is under the chair. (in, on, under, next to, between, behind)
  2. (Esconde un objeto) Where is the ball now? S9It is behind the sofa. It is between the book and the lamp.
  3. What is there in your bedroom? S10There is a bed. There are two lamps. There is a TV next to the window. (is + uno, are + varios)
  4. Is there a TV in your room? Are there any books? S10Yes, there is. / No, there isn't. Yes, there are many books.
  5. Describe your dream room. S11In my dream room there is a big bed. There is a guitar next to my bed. There are three robots.
  6. What places are there in your neighborhood? S14There is a park, a supermarket and a school.
  7. Give me directions from here to the kitchen (o al baño, a la puerta). S13Go straight. Turn left. Stop! The kitchen is next to the living room. (go straight, turn left/right, stop, go back, cross)
  8. Where is the bakery? (muestra el mapa del juego o uno dibujado) S14The bakery is next to the park. / It is between the bank and the school.
  9. Compare: an elephant and a dog. A car and a bike. Pizza and broccoli. S15The elephant is bigger than the dog. A car is faster / more expensive than a bike. Pizza is better than broccoli.
  10. Who is taller, you or your sister/brother? Who is older? S15I am taller than Luciana. Jero is older than me.
  11. Describe this (un objeto) with 3 or 4 words. Remember the order. BonusA beautiful big old blue sofa. (Opinion → Size → Age → Color → noun)

3. Mini charla (1 minuto)

"You are a tour guide. Show me your world." Debe incluir: lo que hay en su casa (there is / there are), dónde están las cosas (preposiciones), cómo llegar a un lugar (direcciones) y una comparación.
